Selling in 2026. Preparing for 2027.

Help customers justify buying now.

Funding remains selective. Farm customers face high costs and pressure on profits. AI is giving them more tools to evaluate. Your sales process needs to answer three questions clearly: what changes in their business, what it costs to get there, and why the purchase should happen now.

01 · The purchase
Customers like the product, but postpone buying.
Make the financial case specific to the customer: savings, implementation costs, payback and cash timing. Focus on buyers with a problem worth solving and a budget to solve it.
02 · The pilot
The pilot worked. The contract hasn’t followed.
Agree what success looks like, who owns the budget and what happens after the trial. Give each pilot a defined purchasing decision before committing more time and resources.
03 · The AI question
“AI-powered” still needs a reason to pay.
Explain the job your product does, the evidence behind its results and how it fits the customer’s operation. Make the benefit clear enough to compare with existing tools and ways of working.
04 · The founder
Every deal still depends on you.
Give your team clear rules for qualifying buyers, recording next steps and moving opportunities forward. Know which deals deserve attention and which are consuming time you cannot afford to lose.
